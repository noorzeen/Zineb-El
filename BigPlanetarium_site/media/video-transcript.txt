Welcome to BigPlanetarium.
In this short video, we travel from the Milky Way toward our home.
The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy filled with stars, gas, and dust.
Our Solar System sits in one of the galaxy’s spiral arms.
As we zoom in, planets orbit the Sun in predictable paths.
Earth is our home world, with liquid water and a protective atmosphere.
Mars is colder and drier, but scientists still search for clues of past water.
Exploring space helps us understand our planet—and our place in the universe.
