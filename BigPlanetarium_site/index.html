What is a galaxy?

A galaxy is a massive system of stars, planets, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity. The Milky Way is one of billions of galaxies in the universe.

Where are we?

Our Solar System sits in a region called the Orion Arm. We orbit the galactic centre along with many other stars.

How do we learn about space?

Scientists use telescopes, satellites, and robotic spacecraft to observe and measure objects far beyond Earth.
